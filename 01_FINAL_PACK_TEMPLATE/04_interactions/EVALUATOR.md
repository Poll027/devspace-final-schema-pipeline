# 04 interactions — EVALUATOR
Fresh context. **Solve every interaction blind first** — from the lesson text only, without reading the key — then compare with the key. The deterministic gate already verified keys against the data; you judge what a script can't.

BLOCKING: more than one defensible answer; a key that is wrong or arguable; a question that needs knowledge no earlier step taught; wrong-option feedback that is false or misleading; a hands-on that does not exercise the practice anchor's `success` sentence; a distractor that is actually correct. ADVISORY: option wording, hint quality, difficulty labels, feedback tone.
Canary tile: every interaction. Later chunks: at least a third (no more than about two thirds unless a defect turns up — then go full depth on that chunk).
End with `VERDICT: PASS|FAIL`, then `BLOCKING|ADVISORY <step_id>: problem → exact fix`.
